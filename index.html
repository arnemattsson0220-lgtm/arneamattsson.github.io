A4 Business Consulting is a Staten Island-based digital marketing and AI automation agency focused on one thing: making local small businesses and contractors impossible to ignore online. We work with roofers, HVAC companies, remodelers, landscapers, and service businesses across Staten Island and the greater NYC area who are serious about growth.

We operate on a "visible proof first" model — we show you what's possible before you commit to anything. No vague pitches, no inflated promises. Every engagement starts with a clear demonstration of what we'll build and exactly how it generates revenue for your business.
